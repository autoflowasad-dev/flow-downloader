Veo AI Video Batch & 4K Automation for Google Flow

AutoFlow: Veo AI Video Batch for Google Flow sends your entire list of prompts to Google Flow automatically, generates high-quality videos and images without manual supervision, and saves every file with numbered names — 001.mp4, 002.mp4, 003.mp4 — keeping long video series in perfect order.

Developed by Muhammad Asad. Version 2.15.2. Independent productivity tool; not affiliated with Google LLC.

Getting started in 4 simple steps

  1. Open your Google Flow project in a Chrome tab.
  2. Open the Auto Flow sidepanel (click the toolbar icon, or press Alt+Shift+F).
  3. Paste your prompts or import a .txt / .csv file.
  4. Select your settings (Veo 3.1, Omni, 4K download) and press Start. Auto Flow handles all clicks, typing, and downloads automatically.

Core Features

🎬 All AI Video & Image Models Omni 1.1 Flash, Veo 3.1 (Lite, Fast, Quality, and Lower Priority models), plus Nano Banana image models with seamless switching.

Reference Pictures & Chaining

Open the Pictures tab to upload character avatars or environment shots:

  • In list order: 1st picture accompanies prompt 1, 2nd picture with prompt 2, etc.
  • By number in filename: 026.jpg automatically attaches to Prompt 26.
  • Frame Chaining: Automatically take the last frame of the previous video as the starting frame of the next one.
  • Inline prompt tags: [REF: hero.png], [START: a.png], [CHAR: Ali].

Frequently Asked Questions

Google Flow ran out of credits?
Auto Flow automatically detects the orange Insufficient credits warning, cleanly pauses the batch, and waits for you to press Resume once credits are replenished.

A file missed downloading?
Open the Flow project and click Save missing files in the panel. Auto Flow finds and downloads any missing outputs.

Can I pause and change models?
Yes, press Pause (it stops immediately), adjust your settings in the panel, and press Resume.
