Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

Clear, transparent disclosure of how Flow Media Downloader protects your privacy, handles browser permissions, and ensures all video and picture downloads remain strictly on your local computer.

Version 1.0.0 · Last Updated: September 2026 · Developer: Muhammad Asad

🛡️ Privacy at a Glance

• Fully Local On-Device Processing: Flow Downloader runs entirely in your local browser sandbox. It detects generated media on flow.google.com and saves files directly to your disk via Chrome's native download API.

• Zero Data Harvesting: Your video clips, images, text prompts, and project files are NEVER uploaded, monitored, or transmitted to any external server.

• Optional Activation: Providing an email is completely optional and can be skipped at any time. There are no passwords, no paywalls, and no subscription fees.

1 Overview & Standalone Architecture

Flow Media Downloader is a specialized Chrome extension engineered to help digital creators, filmmakers, and AI artists save their generated videos and pictures from Google Flow (flow.google.com) with clean sequential numbering and upscale interception.

Unlike cloud scrapers or third-party web converters, Flow Downloader uses a client-only architecture. The extension interacts with the open Google Flow tab in your browser and triggers downloads directly through Chrome's background API. No intermediate servers or proxies ever sit between Google Flow and your machine.

2 Free Access & No Monetization of Data

Flow Media Downloader is free to use. There are no paid tiers, no credits, no token deductions, and no hidden subscriptions.

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to data brokers, advertisers, or analytics networks.
  • We do not inject ads, sponsored links, or affiliate tracking cookies into your browsing sessions.
  • We do not restrict download volume. You can download 1 file or 10,000 files completely free.

3 Optional User Email & Communications

On initial launch, Flow Downloader provides an optional activation screen where users may enter their email address:

  • Completely Optional: You may click "Skip" to begin downloading immediately with full functionality. Entering an email is never required.
  • Purpose of Collection: If you choose to enter your email, it is used solely by developer Muhammad Asad to provide product support, notify you of major Google Flow compatibility updates, and share workflow tips.
  • Opt-Out & Removal: You can change or clear your email at any time directly in the Side Panel by clicking "Change" on the registered bar, or by emailing autoflow.asad@gmail.com with subject "Unsubscribe".

4 Google Flow Canvas & Media Handling

When you use the extension on Google Flow:

  • Local DOM Detection: The extension inspects Google Flow's virtual scroller container to identify tile media URLs (video .mp4 URLs and picture .png/.webp URLs) that Google Flow has already generated for you.
  • Zero Prompt Harvesting: While tile prompts may be displayed in the Side Panel list to help you identify which clip is which before deleting or downloading, prompts are never transmitted externally.
  • No Account Access: The extension does NOT read, store, or intercept your Google login credentials, cookies, payment methods, or personal Google profile info.

5 Browser Storage (chrome.storage.local)

Flow Downloader saves minimal configuration data locally on your computer using Chrome's secure storage API:

  • fd_settings: Your preferred download options (e.g. 4K upscale, folder name, number padding, prefix).
  • fd_saved_keys: Unique tile hashes of items you have downloaded, so the "Save Missing Files" and "Skip files already downloaded" features know which items are already on your hard drive.
  • fd_activation: Your optional email address if entered.

All stored data lives exclusively on your local machine and is wiped automatically if you uninstall the extension or clear your Chrome browsing data.

6 Manifest V3 Permissions Justification

In adherence to Google Chrome's strict Manifest V3 Least-Privilege guidelines, each requested permission serves an explicit, user-facing function:

Permission Technical Purpose
storage Saves your download settings and completed item history locally.
sidePanel Renders the side-by-side management panel next to your active Google Flow window.
downloads Directs video and image files into your Downloads directory with sequential filenames (001.mp4, etc.).
tabs Detects whether you are currently viewing a Google Flow project tab to update the connection status.
power (optional) Keeps your system awake during large batch downloads so long queues are not cut off by sleep mode.
notifications (optional) Notifies you when a long batch download completes.

We implement industry-standard security practices:
